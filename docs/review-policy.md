# Review Policy

## Independent Honest Reporting

All hardware reviews and compatibility reports are conducted and published independently. The goal is to provide accurate, useful information to the community.

### Transparency and Disclosures

Always disclose the following **prominently** at the top of every review or report:

- **Free samples** — whether the hardware was provided at no cost
- **Loans** — whether the hardware was borrowed and must be returned
- **Discounts** — whether a purchase price was reduced
- **Sponsorship** — any financial support received
- **Affiliate links** — any links that generate commission
- **Conflicts of interest** — any relationship with the manufacturer, competitor, or related parties

### Coverage Guarantees

- **No guaranteed positive coverage** — reviews reflect the actual evaluation outcome
- Hardware that performs poorly or has significant limitations will be reported as such
- Positive reviews are earned through genuine performance, not purchased

### Manufacturer Interaction

- **Factual corrections allowed** — manufacturers may point out verifiable factual errors in a review, which will be corrected
- **No editorial veto** — manufacturers cannot dictate review content, conclusions, or whether a review is published
- Manufacturers cannot require pre-publication approval of reviews

### Publication Standards

- **Publish failures and limitations** — issues, bugs, and shortcomings are documented alongside positive findings
- **Clearly label untested or planned work** — mark sections as "Not Tested" or "Planned" when work has not been completed
- Avoid speculation presented as fact
- Distinguish between measured data, vendor claims, and personal opinions

### Conflict Resolution

If a conflict arises between reviewer independence and external pressure, **reviewer independence always takes priority**. The disclosure section should be expanded to describe any attempted pressure.
